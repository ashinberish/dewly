Interaction

Why it feels like iOS.

Following Apple's Designing Fluid Interfaces guidance, the prototype uses the same motion techniques as iOS.

Instant response

Buttons shrink the moment your finger touches them, before you let go. Nothing waits for a tap to finish.

Springs, not timers

The ring, the tab highlight, the segmented control and the sheet all move on springs. Tap quickly and the motion picks up from where it is, with no jumps.

Throw to dismiss

The sheet follows your finger exactly. When you let go, its speed decides where it lands, and dragging it upward meets gentle resistance.

Consistent origins

Notifications grow out of the Dynamic Island and leave the same way. The sheet rises from the bottom and returns there.

Forgiveness

Every log shows an Undo toast, so there's never a “Are you sure?” dialog.

Accessible by default

Respects Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency, supports dark mode, and every control is a large, rounded target.
